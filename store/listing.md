```
The graveyard is pitch dark. Your lantern is everything.

You are the night keeper, and the dead are rising. Your weapons fire on their own: you steer with one thumb, gather oil to keep your lantern burning, and choose a new upgrade every time you level up. Survive the shift until dawn breaks and the sunrise burns the horde away.

THE HOOK: YOUR LANTERN
• Light is your lifeline. Let the oil run out and the darkness closes in
• Every level-up makes the lantern flare
• See what is coming before it reaches you

BUILD YOUR LOADOUT
• Level up and pick from random upgrades: soul blasters, orbiting jack-o'-lanterns, falling tombstones, holy grenades and more
• Stack upgrades to grow wild builds every run
• Spend gold between runs on permanent upgrades

BOSSES AND A SECOND LEVEL
• Face graveyard bosses, from a giant zombie to a vampire lord
• Beat all three graveyard bosses to unlock the BLOOD MOON level
• Six bosses across two levels, plus treasure chests and revives

MADE FOR PHONES
• One-thumb controls: drag anywhere to move
• Plays offline, no account needed
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected
• Pauses itself when you leave the app; Android back pauses too

Can you keep your lantern lit until dawn?
```
